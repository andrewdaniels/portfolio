Sharing got a full redesign in v2. Share any brew as a card with its recipe, pour map, rating and tasting notes, on a background you pick. At the end of the year, Brew Review sums up your year in coffee. The layouts are also tuned for the iPhone Duo, and the new extraction tool plots your yield and TDS against the ideal range.
