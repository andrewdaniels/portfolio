I'm building Uncomn end to end: product strategy, the design system, the iOS app and the web platform.

- **Home Screen widgets:** Next Up, Organization Stats, Attendance and Group Health, so leaders see what's next without opening the app.
- **Automatic events** from recurring meetings, with reminders and RSVPs by push and email.
- **Curriculum and lessons,** with the week's scripture and assignment in one place.
- **Attendance and group health** for leaders and church admins, including show rates by group.
- **Organization announcements:** admins post once, and it lands on every group's home page as a push.
- **Threaded replies** on each group's message board.
