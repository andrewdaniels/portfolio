I'm building Uncomn end to end: product strategy, design system, the iOS app and the web platform.

- **Automatic events** from recurring meetings, with reminders and RSVPs by push and email
- **Curriculum and lessons**, with the week's scripture and assignment in one place
- **Attendance and group health** views for leaders and church admins
- **Threaded group messaging** and join-request management
- **iOS home-screen widgets** that show the next meeting, lesson and checklist without opening the app
