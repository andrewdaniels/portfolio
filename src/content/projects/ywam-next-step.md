## The problem

Most YWAM locations don't have staff with every skill they need, while many YWAMers have skills nobody near them can use. Next Step connects the two, remotely and at no cost, and needed a site that could also launch a product.

## What I built

- **Two doors on one homepage.** YWAMers with skills to share, and locations with needs, each get a clear way in.
- **A launch page for YWAM Base.** A tour through every module, from websites and calendars to accounting, forms and scheduling, each with a real product screen.
- **One design language.** The site uses the YWAM Base blue and type, so the site and the product feel like one place.
