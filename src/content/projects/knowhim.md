V2 rebuilds KnowHim around doing faith with other people. Every part of the app is made to be shared: you can take a course with a friend, pray in a circle that keeps each other on track, and gather your small group in one place.

- **Courses:** short daily lessons from Genesis to Revelation, about four minutes each. You can see your friends' progress and talk about each lesson in a course chat.
- **Prayer:** reminders on the days you choose, prayer lists that show how often you've prayed for each request, notes and journaling, and a record of answered prayers.
- **Prayer circles:** shared weekly goals, a view of who prayed today, and a nudge for friends who haven't.
- **Groups:** meeting times, prayer requests, notes and chat for church small groups.
- **Streaks and reviews:** daily and weekly streaks, plus weekly and monthly recaps.
- **Widgets:** your prayer group, today's verse and your next lesson on the iOS Home Screen.

The first version shipped 157 screens in three months on FlutterFlow with a Firebase backend. V2 moves the app to Flutter.
