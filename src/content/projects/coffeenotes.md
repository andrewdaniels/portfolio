I started Coffee Notes as a side project to dial in my own brews, and it grew into a small social app.

- **Brew notes:** dose, water, ratio, equipment, grinder, grind size, brew time and tasting notes, plus a rating.
- **My Coffees:** the bags you're brewing now and everything you've tried, with roaster and roast date.
- **Coffee tools:** a ratio calculator and a brew-map timer that walks you through each pour.
- **Friends:** a feed of what friends are brewing, with likes, comments and their brew maps.

It was built in FlutterFlow on Firebase and is now moving to plain Flutter.
