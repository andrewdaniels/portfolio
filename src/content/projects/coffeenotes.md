Coffee Notes started as a way to remember how I brewed a coffee I loved, so I could make it again. It's grown into a small social app for home brewers on the App Store.

## What it does

- **Log your brews:** coffee, method, grind, ratio, water temperature and brew time, plus a rating and notes.
- **Make it again:** every brew is saved with its recipe, so a great cup is easy to repeat.
- **Brew with friends:** follow friends, see what they're brewing, and like and comment on their posts.
- **See your trends:** your ratings over time, your favorite coffees and roasters, and how you brew each month.

## What's new in version 2

- **Faster to add a coffee:** paste a link to the coffee from the roaster's site, or scan the bag, and the details fill in for you.
- **Better coffee tools:** a new extraction calculator, plus a cleaner layout for the brew timer and ratio calculator.
