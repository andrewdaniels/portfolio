## The problem

Most visitors are deciding whether to give five months and a few thousand euros to a place they've never seen. They want dates, costs and what a week looks like. The base had also just raised $1.175M for a 10-bedroom hotel and needed to tell that story to supporters.

## What I built

- **One clear next step.** The next school's dates, the deadline and an Apply button sit above every page.
- **Facts before the pitch.** The next school, the apply-by date and the length of the lecture and outreach phases sit right under the hero.
- **The fees, in full.** Every euro is listed, €6,040 in total, before anyone has to ask.
- **Two languages.** The whole site switches between English and Spanish.

## How it was built

I designed the site and built it with **Claude**: hand-written HTML and CSS with a five-colour palette and no framework. The code and every change live in **GitHub**, and **Cloudflare** puts each push live. [Read how I build sites with Claude →](/blog/build-your-website-with-claude)
