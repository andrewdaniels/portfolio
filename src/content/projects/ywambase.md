## The problem

Running a YWAM base means juggling schools, students, staff, budgets, kitchens, vehicles and buildings, usually across a dozen disconnected tools. Information lives in spreadsheets, group chats, task boards and shared drives, so the answer to a simple question ("who's driving to the airport Friday?" or "what does this student still owe?") takes a round of messages.

## The product

YWAM Base brings every part of base life into one place:

- **Schools & students:** school pages, student records and filterable saved views.
- **Planning:** a timeline for the year across schools and events.
- **Budgeting & finances:** budgets, student balances, invoices and support gifts.
- **Maintenance:** tickets for buildings and grounds.
- **Transportation:** drag to book vehicles.
- **Meals:** menus and meal-credit headcounts for the kitchen.
- **Community:** calendar, chat, announcements, a feed, hospitality, work duties, rooms and appointments.

## Design decisions

- **Role-aware navigation.** The sidebar adapts to each person's role, so a school leader, a kitchen lead and a driver each see what's relevant to them.
- **Modules per base.** Every base is different, so modules can be switched on or off per base.
- **Fast setup.** Staff are invited by email and existing spreadsheets can be imported, so a base can be set up in an afternoon.
- **Everywhere.** It runs in the browser on computers, tablets and phones, with light and dark modes.
