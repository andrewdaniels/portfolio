My portfolio used to live in Webflow: a CMS full of case studies, a designer I had to open for every change, and custom scripts pasted into the project settings.

Now it's a small Astro site. Every case study, post and work update is a Markdown file in a Git repo, and Netlify redeploys the site whenever something changes. That means I can ask Claude to "add a post about the Uncomn widgets" or "move KnowHim to the top" and it edits the files directly.

## What changed

- **Building now comes first.** Uncomn and KnowHim are the products I'm putting my time into, so they lead the site.
- **A feed instead of long case studies.** Most work is easier to understand as a stream of screens and short notes than as a 3,000-word write-up.
- **About and Contact are sheets, not pages.** They open over whatever you're reading, and you can close them to pick up where you left off.
